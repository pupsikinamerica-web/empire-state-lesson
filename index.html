A Proposal 443 metres above New York

On the afternoon of July 1, 2026, people in Midtown Manhattan looked up and could not believe their eyes. Two small figures, dressed in black and wearing masks, were standing at the very top of the Empire State Building — not on the observation deck, but on the antenna itself, 443 metres (1,454 feet) above the street. They had no safety ropes. Then they did something even more surprising: they unfurled a huge black banner with white letters.

“When the power of love beats the love of power, the world knows peace.”
The words on the banner — a quote often connected to Jimi Hendrix

The two climbers were Angela Nikolau, 33, and Ivan Kuznetsov, 32, better known online as Ivan Beerkus. They are a daredevil couple from Russia and famous rooftoppers — people who climb tall buildings without permission and take incredible photos at the top. They have been together since 2016, and their love story became the 2024 Netflix documentary Skywalkers: A Love Story. In the film, they climb some of the tallest buildings in the world, including Merdeka 118 in Malaysia.

But this climb was special. After they took down the banner, Ivan got down on one knee and proposed to Angela. She said yes. Later she posted photos of a ring on her hand. When a police officer finally reached them and said, “Well, you can’t be up here,” the couple answered simply: “We are engaged.”

How did they get there? According to the police, the couple went through a hatch on the 103rd floor — a technical area that is closed to the public — and then climbed the metal structure of the antenna by hand. The situation was extremely dangerous: the antenna sends out powerful radio signals that can harm the human body. The police had to switch off the antenna, which took about 30 minutes, before officers could climb 380 metres up to rescue the couple. The streets below were closed, and the observation deck was evacuated.

Back on the ground, the romance met reality. Police arrested Angela and Ivan, and they now face serious charges, including reckless endangerment, trespassing and burglary. The police even confiscated the engagement ring. The couple were released the next day. Leaving the court, Ivan said only four words: “We believe in love.”

The internet exploded. Some people called the stunt the most romantic proposal in history. Others said it was selfish and put police officers in danger. The Empire State Building itself joked on social media that it offers “a practical way for the most memorable marriage proposals” — on the observation deck, of course. One thing is certain: nobody will forget this story soon.
